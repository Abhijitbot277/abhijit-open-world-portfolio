Curious about technology.

I'm Abhijit Singha, a first-year B.Tech Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at Swami Vivekananda Institute of Science & Technology (SVIST), under MAKAUT. My engineering background gives me an interest in how technology works, while my curiosity has led me toward software, artificial intelligence, digital design and practical problem solving.

I enjoy learning by building rather than only studying concepts. I explore ideas through personal projects, hackathon concepts and experiments with AI-assisted tools. I value consistency, curiosity and practical experience, and I'm still early in my journey — building skills one project at a time.
